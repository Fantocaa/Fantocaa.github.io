# Company Profiles and Websites

I worked on the following company websites and landing pages. The portfolio includes Next.js sites, some with an Express backend, hosted on private company servers.

## Company profiles

### PT Anugerah Trans Maritim (ATM)

A company profile for logistics and shipping services, including door-to-door, port-to-door, door-to-port, and port-to-port delivery. [Visit ATM](https://atmtrans.id/).

### PT PINT Indonesia Logistik

A company website introducing sea, land, and multimodal shipping services. [Visit PINT Logistik](https://pintlogistik.com/).

### PT Anugerah Multi Logistik

A company profile for domestic and international freight, customs clearance, trucking, and multimodal transport. [Visit AM Logistik](https://amlogistik.com/).

### PT Armada Berkat Anugerah

A website for local and long-distance trucking services for different shipment sizes. [Visit Armada Berkat](https://armadaberkat.com/).

### PT Berlimpah Anugerah Sejati

### PT Anugerah Jelajah Indonesia Logistic (AJI Logistic)

A website for domestic sea freight, truck rental, warehousing, and shipment tracking. [Visit AJI Logistic](https://ajilogistic.com/).

### PT Anugerah Mandiri Internasional Trans (AMIN TRANS)

### PT Caritravel Indonesia

### PT Cahaya Mercusuar Indonesia

### CV Lima Roti Dua Ikan (Takopi)
