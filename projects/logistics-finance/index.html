Multi-Tenant Trading & Finance System

A multi-tenant system for managing trading operations and financial activity, from day-to-day transactions through to balance sheets and profit-and-loss reports.

The work

The system connects trading operations with financial management. I worked on invoice payment calculations, receivables and payables, settlement corrections, and cash and bank reporting. Reports include opening and running balances, date and account filters, and PDF or spreadsheet exports.

The application uses Laravel, React, and TypeScript. Its multi-tenant model supports multiple organizations.

Application dashboard

The dashboard brings financial summaries, inventory value, and sales and purchase trends together in one view.

Visit the application

Open the MTI application. The application is self-hosted on a private company server and requires a login; the source code is private.

Sistem Trading & Keuangan Multi-Tenant

Sistem multi-tenant untuk mengelola operasional trading dan aktivitas keuangan, mulai dari transaksi sehari-hari hingga laporan neraca dan laba rugi.

Pekerjaan yang dilakukan

Sistem ini menghubungkan operasional trading dengan pengelolaan keuangan. Saya mengerjakan perhitungan pembayaran faktur, piutang dan utang, koreksi pelunasan, serta laporan kas dan bank. Laporan mencakup saldo awal dan saldo berjalan, filter tanggal dan akun, serta ekspor PDF atau spreadsheet.

Aplikasi menggunakan Laravel, React, dan TypeScript. Model multi-tenant mendukung penggunaan oleh beberapa organisasi.

Tampilan dashboard

Dashboard menyatukan ringkasan keuangan, nilai persediaan, serta tren penjualan dan pembelian dalam satu tampilan.

Kunjungi aplikasi

Buka aplikasi MTI. Aplikasi di-host sendiri pada server pribadi perusahaan dan memerlukan login; kode sumber bersifat privat.
